# NICKED owner setup and review

This is the staging checklist. Fields marked `OWNER TO CONFIRM` remain private or disabled until reviewed.

## Catalogue review

- For each of the nine products, confirm Yes / No for sulfate-free, silicone-free, and paraben-free claims. Product ingredient lists contain conflicts with some printed free-from claims, so the plugin keeps these claims private until you approve them.
- Supply the corrected ingredients for **Tea Tree Rejuvenating Shampoo**. Its printed ingredients duplicate the Marula shampoo list.
- Supply a clean approved overview and ingredient list for **Tea Tree Rejuvenating Conditioner**. Its printed copy is garbled or unclear.
- Supply the missing how-to-use directions for **Post-Treatment Shampoo**.
- Provide evidence before publishing the Heat Protection Spray’s 235°C, UV, or “scientifically formulated” claims.
- Ask a qualified formulator to review methylchloroisothiazolinone and methylisothiazolinone in both leave-on sprays before publishing those ingredient lists.
- Review all product names, sizes, MRP, routine groupings, photographs, summaries, directions, and ingredients before changing a draft product to Published.

## Business and customer information

- Legal business name and address
- GSTIN and HSN codes
- Manufacturer / packer details and country of origin
- Support phone, WhatsApp, and email
- Social profile links
- Grievance contact

## Store operations

- Delivery regions, shipping rates, and any free-shipping rule
- Return, refund, cancellation, privacy, and terms copy; approve and publish each policy when ready
- Stock counts, product weights, and dimensions
- Cash on Delivery availability
- Subscription-eligible products, intervals, discounts, and Razorpay Subscriptions status
- WooCommerce tax configuration and the treatment of printed MRP
- Payment, shipping, transactional email, refund, and order-tracking configuration

## Admin panel

Use **Products → Add new** in WordPress to add products, or edit and remove products there. The NICKED Overview dashboard summarizes sales, refunds, order statuses, stock, recent orders, best sellers, and subscriptions. Products with an order history should be archived to preserve the order record.
